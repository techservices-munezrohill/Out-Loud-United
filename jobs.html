Why Work With Out Loud & United?

Meaningful Impact

Work directly on advancing LGBTQ+ rights and creating positive change in Rwanda's communities.

Inclusive Environment

Join a diverse, supportive team that values different perspectives and lived experiences.

Professional Growth

Develop your skills while working on challenging, impactful projects in advocacy and social justice.

How to Apply

Application Requirements

  • Updated CV/Resume
  • Cover letter explaining your interest and relevant experience
  • Contact information for 2-3 professional references
  • Portfolio or work samples (where applicable)

Our Process

  1. Application Review (1-2 weeks)
  2. Initial Interview (Phone/Video)
  3. In-person/Final Interview
  4. Reference Check
  5. Job Offer and Onboarding
